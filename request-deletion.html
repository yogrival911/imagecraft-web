Delete Your Account

This page allows users of AI Hairstyle, Haircut & Colour (Android app, package name: com.yogdroidtech.hairstylistai), developed by yogdroidtech, to request deletion of their account and associated data.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data using this page.

How to request account deletion

  1. Enter the email address associated with your account below.
  2. Click Send deletion link.
  3. Check your email for the account deletion confirmation message.
  4. Click the Delete My Account link in the confirmation email.
  5. Your account and associated data will then be permanently deleted.

For security, your account will only be deleted after you confirm the deletion request using the link sent to your email address.

Data that will be deleted

After you confirm the deletion request, the following account-associated data will be deleted:

  • Account and profile information
  • Uploaded photos
  • Generated hairstyle, haircut, and colour preview images
  • Credit balance and account-related credit data
  • Other personal data associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law, for financial or transaction records, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal requirements.

Any information that must be retained will only be kept for the period required for the applicable purpose and will be deleted when it is no longer necessary.

Important

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account and associated data have been deleted, they cannot be recovered.
