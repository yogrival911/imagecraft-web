Ghar Saaf Karo is a casual game where you tidy up rooms by dragging household items to their right place. This policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled. In short: we do not collect your name, email or any other personal details. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it has no sign-up or login. We do not run our own servers and receive no data about you from the game.

Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a small amount of game data in your phone's local app storage:

  • Levels completed, stars earned and in-game coins
  • The date of your last daily reward and your reward streak
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration and the language you picked

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback when you pick up and place items. You can turn it off in the game's settings.
  • Internet: used to load ads. The game itself works offline; ads simply don't appear when you have no connection.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): lets Google AdMob show relevant ads and limit how often you see the same one. You can reset or delete it in your phone's Google settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ghar Saaf Karo is free because it shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location may be worked out
  • Basic device and app information, and how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data of the ad SDK

Some ads are optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch them in return for coins, extra time or a doubled daily reward. Other ads may appear between levels from time to time.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time under Settings → Privacy settings in the game.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience. Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn off ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

Other third-party services

Apart from Google AdMob, the game does not include analytics, crash reporting or social media services.

Coins and purchases

Coins are earned by playing or by watching optional rewarded ads, and have no real-money value. The game has no in-app purchases and asks for no payment information.

Children's privacy

Ghar Saaf Karo is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the game, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Security

Your game progress is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Data used for ads is sent by the Google AdMob SDK to Google over encrypted connections and is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding new features that use data, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top before the change goes live.
