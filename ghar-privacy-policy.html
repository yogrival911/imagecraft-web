Ghar Saaf Karo is a casual game where you tidy up rooms by dragging household items to their right place. This policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled. In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it has no sign-up or login.

Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a small amount of game data in your phone's local app storage:

  • Levels completed, stars earned and in-game coins
  • The date of your last daily reward and your reward streak
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration and the language you picked

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback when you pick up and place items. You can turn it off in the game's settings.
  • Internet: included by the app framework. The game does not use it to send your data anywhere and plays fully offline.

Third-party services

The game does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media services, and it does not share data with any third party.

Coins and purchases

Coins are earned only by playing and have no real-money value. The game has no in-app purchases and asks for no payment information.

Children's privacy

Ghar Saaf Karo is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has somehow shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your game progress is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Since no data is sent over the internet, there is nothing for us to store or protect on a server.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding new features that use data, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top before the change goes live.
